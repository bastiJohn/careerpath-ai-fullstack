Our Mission

CareerPath AI will help address the challenges that come with choosing a Senior High School track.

CareerPath AI aims to help students find the track and strand that best suit their interests, skills, and goals by providing a personalized Senior High School guidance tool that is accessible to all Grade 10 students. It empowers them to make informed and confident choices regarding their education and future careers.

DepEd Alignment

CareerPath AI is aligned with Republic Act No. 11206, or the Secondary School Guidance and Counseling Act, which establishes a mandatory career guidance and counseling program to guide students in choosing their tertiary education path.

In this case, CareerPath AI helps students choose which track they will pursue in Senior High School by considering factors aligned with their strengths, weaknesses, and academic performance. CareerPath AI provides choices that match their skills and interests and also gives suggestions on possible college courses and schools they may pursue after Senior High School.

CareerPath AI will serve as a technological support tool for guidance counselors, helping them address the difficulties faced by secondary students who are having difficulty choosing the right educational and career path for them.
